Designed for real projects
Automation with visible boundaries.
Local by construction
  • Unity listens only on 127.0.0.1 with a same-user registry token.
  • Multi-client write lease prevents simultaneous mutation.
  • Immediate local kill switch closes clients, jobs and discovery.
Human-gated publishing
  • VRChat upload has no AI-callable arm method.
  • One local click arms one upload for at most 30 minutes.
  • Creator Console exposes compile errors, job state and connected PIDs.
